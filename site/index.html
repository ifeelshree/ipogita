    Before you apply

    1. Check if the issue is mostly fresh capital or an offer for sale (promoters selling).
    2. Look at subscription by category on the last day, not on day one.
    3. Read the GMP trend, not one day's number. A falling premium into close is a warning.
    4. SME issues: small lots, thin trading, wide swings. Size your bet accordingly.
    5. Read the RHP for the use of proceeds, debt and promoter holding.
